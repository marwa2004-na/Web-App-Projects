About US!

Do you know our bakery?

If no then you must be curious about us !

We are the most famous bakery in paris as well as other parts of the world.Our story begins with our great chef Cédric Grolet when he decides to open his first bakery/pastery shop in Le Meurice in Paris (March 2018),After that the number of customers started to spike hugely,So we opened our second gallery in paris in 2019, As our cheif is so passionate about his job he opened his third bakery in London (2022), and recently a fourth shop in Singapore.
